The tables

Every figure on this page is a cell in one of these files, written by the build from the publishers' releases and never edited by hand. Each view names the tables it reads; the checks file records what each table was tested for and the value it measured.

Ink on paper, one splash of colour in each view, a little life now and then, a gull, a plane on the approach, a rocket out of the south, a robotaxi that meets a hydrant, a gust, and the sky drawn from the latest observation at the airport: the description and visibility pick one of eight presets, wind sets how fast the fog and cloud drift and which way, by the heading of the view you are looking along, and rain falls slanted by it. The light follows San Francisco's clock: the sun rises over the city and sets past the bridge, the drawing turns to white ink on black after dark, and the moon is drawn at its phase. Nothing in the drawing encodes a number from the index; those sit beneath it in their own units, with the file each came from.
