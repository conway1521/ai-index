Where usage lands

Three views from one point

Every AI tracker asks whether people are using it. This one asks what that use has reached: the money paid for the tasks, the pay and jobs behind them, and the people training to do them. Each view is a measurement with its own units, never a score.

Ink on paper is the whole visual language: the lighthouse, the fog, the city, the bridge and the valley are drawn. The weather is the one live thing in the picture: fog thickness follows the visibility at the airport, the fog drifts with the wind, cloud and rain are drawn when they are reported, so the page shows the sky over the bay at the moment it loads. Nothing in the drawing encodes a number from the index; those sit beneath it in their own units, with the file each came from.
